How many totes do I need?

A rough guide: 20 totes covers a 1-bedroom apartment, 40 covers a 2–3 bedroom home, and 60 covers 4+ bedrooms. When in doubt, go one size up. If you run short mid-move, call us and we'll work something out.

Are you a moving company?

No. We're a tote rental service. We deliver clean totes and a dolly to your door and pick them up when you're done. You handle the packing and moving yourself (or hire movers separately).

What if I need the totes longer than 2 weeks?

No problem. Contact us before your scheduled pickup and we'll arrange an extension for an additional fee. Just let us know ahead of time, not after the pickup date.

What can I put in the totes?

Normal household and office items. Each tote holds up to 65 lbs. Don't use them for hazardous materials, chemicals, liquids that can spill, perishable food, or anything illegal. Use padding for fragile items.

What happens if a tote is lost or damaged?

Normal wear is expected and fine. Lost, stolen, or damaged-beyond-use totes are charged at a reasonable replacement cost. We'll communicate that cost upfront if it ever comes up.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Cancel or reschedule at no charge up to 48 hours before your scheduled delivery. Within 48 hours, a fee may apply. Just call or text us.

Do you deliver to my area?

We offer free delivery across the OKC metro (30-mile radius) and the Ada area (15-mile radius). Check the maps below, or text us your zip code and we'll tell you right away.

Can I pick up the totes myself?

Yes! If you prefer to pick up and return the totes yourself, we offer that option. Just let us know when you book.

Can I get same-day or rush delivery?

Yes, subject to availability. Requests within 24 hours of delivery include a $25 expedite fee. We'll confirm the total before invoicing.
