The Dashboard is home base. Needs you at the top lists anything waiting: requests, late pickups, totes to clean, partner follow-ups, trips to log. Tap a line to go fix it.

1. A new booking request comes in

You get a phone alert. The request waits on the Requests page. The booking form only asks for the package, delivery date, address and contact info; a time of day and promo codes are optional, so many requests have no time picked.

  1. Open Requests. Check the Stock line: green means you have the totes, red means you're short on those dates.
  2. Tap Text to confirm. Messages opens with the text, their dates and times, and a confirm link filled in. Send it.
  3. That's it: the request has already moved to Awaiting customer. Didn't send it after all? Tap Undo on the card.

When the customer taps the link and confirms:

  • Standard booking (regular package, 2 weeks, no rush, no military or promo discount, full address, totes free): the invoice is emailed right away and they get a Pay now button on the spot. You get a Booking confirmed and invoiced alert. Nothing else to do.
  • Anything else: you get a Booking confirmed alert with the reason, and the request shows Confirmed for you to invoice (step 2).

If they reply YES by text instead, tap Mark confirmed yourself. Automatic invoices can be turned off on App & alerts.

The confirm text and page show their times: the time of day they picked, or "around 10 AM" if they didn't pick one. Each card also shows Heard via when they told us how they found you.

Can't do it? Tap Decline and pick a reason. Not sure yet? Mark reviewed keeps it on your list without a reply. Test booking or spam? Tap Decline, then Test or spam (delete). It's removed from Requests and Contacts in one tap, with an Undo. Older declined requests have a Delete button on the Declined tab.
They reply with a different time? Not invoiced yet: tap Edit details on the request and change the time. Already invoiced: on All Rentals, open their rental, tap Edit rental and change the drop-off or pickup time. Reminder texts use the new time from then on.

2. Send the invoice

Only needed when it wasn't invoiced automatically (step 1).

  1. On the confirmed request, tap Create invoice. Send Invoice opens with everything filled in from the request.
  2. Check the package, dates, address and Total price. Rush fee, military and partner discounts are worked out for you.
  3. Tap Review and send, check the preview, and send. The customer gets the invoice email with a Stripe pay button, and the rental is added to your list.
Customer wants more time? On All Rentals, open their rental and tap Extend rental. Send Invoice fills in the extension (starting on their pickup day, 2 more weeks). The totes count once, the first rental gets no pickup and the extension no delivery. If you start on Send Invoice instead, it asks "Is this an extension?" when that customer still has totes out.
Paid another way, or an old rental? Use Add Rental (Send Invoice, "Log only, no email"). It adds the rental without emailing anyone.

3. Send the day's texts (9 AM)

Delivery and pickup reminders, address checks and payment nudges. They come from your own phone number, so replies land in your Messages.

  1. On the Dashboard (Texts to send today) or the Reminders page, tap Text [name]. Messages opens with the text ready.
  2. Send it. Tapping Text already marks it sent, so there's nothing to tap when you come back. Didn't send it after all? Tap Undo.
  3. Two reminders for the same person (say, a delivery reminder and an invoice follow-up) come as one text, with one Text button.
  4. Don't need one? Tap Skip.

Anything you miss stays on Reminders, marked Overdue, until it's sent, skipped, or too late to matter. Review asks show up 3 days after pickup; send those around 6 PM.

Confirm pickup address texts include a link. The customer taps it and confirms the address or types a new one; that address check then drops off your list. A new address updates the rental and sends you a Pickup address changed alert.

Day-before delivery and pickup reminders end with "If that time doesn't work, reply here and we'll adjust." If someone replies with a new time, change it with Edit rental on All Rentals.

4. Delivery and pickup day

Today's stops are on the Dashboard under Next 7 days. Tap the address to open Maps, or the name to call.

  1. Leaving for a stop? Tap On the way. The app works out your arrival time and fills in a text to the customer. Send it.
  2. Totes dropped off? Tap Delivered. Totes collected? Tap Picked up. Each has an Undo.

Why tap them? The trip's round-trip miles from home are logged on Finance for you, doubled for rentals over 40 totes since that takes two cars (Undo takes it back), marked stops stop sending reminder texts and alerts, the tote counts stay right, and a pickup that's past its time without Picked up shows as a Late pickup in Needs you.

5. Getting paid

  • Paid with the Stripe button: it's marked paid on its own, and you get a phone alert.
  • Paid cash, Venmo or check: tap Mark paid on the Dashboard's Unpaid invoices (or on All Rentals).
  • Still not paid? The app lines up a friendly follow-up text 3 days after the invoice. On day 5 the payment reminder email goes out by itself, and the morning email tells you who got one (switch it off under Automation on App & alerts). Older overdue invoices wait on Reminders; Email reminder sends one any time.

6. Cleaning totes after a pickup

Every rental that comes back shows under Tote cleaning at the top of Inventory until it's washed.

  1. Washed them all? Tap All clean.
  2. Washed only part of a big return? Tap Some, type how many are done so far, and tap Save. The row shows how many are left.
  3. Mis-tap? Tap Undo in the green bar, or next to the name under Washed today.

7. Logging a stock change

Rentals move totes in and out on their own. This is only for the totes you own changing: new ones bought, broken, lost, or worn out.

  1. On Inventory, scroll to Log a stock change. Pick Totes or Dollies.
  2. Set the date, what happened, and how many. Add a note if it helps (an order number, which customer).
  3. Tap Add stock change. It shows in History, where you can delete it if it was a mistake.

Free today is totes you can still book today. Out with customers is totes at customers' homes right now. The 60-day chart shows what's free each day ahead; tap a day to see who has totes out.

The other pages

  • All Rentals: find any rental, edit it, cancel it, add photos, and see what's next for it.
  • Contacts: everyone who rented or asked, with their history, what they owe, how they heard about you, and Text / Email buttons. The line at the top counts how people found you. Don't need to see someone? Open them and tap Hide from Contacts; they come back if they rent or ask again.
  • Finance: money in and out, expenses with receipt photos, mileage (trips log themselves when you mark a stop done; anything missed shows on the Mileage tab to tap and fill in), and a tax set-aside. Download a year for your accountant.
  • Outreach: partners like movers, realtors and remodelers, with follow-up reminders and partner discount codes.
  • App & alerts: put the app on your Home Screen and pick which phone alerts you get. Each phone is set up on its own. Under Automation, switch off automatic invoices, trip miles or payment reminder emails if you ever need to.

If something's off

No morning email by 7:30 AM
It should come every day, even quiet ones. Check spam first; if it's not there, something's wrong, so get it looked at.
Numbers look stale
Tap Refresh at the top of the page.
The app asks you to sign in
Sign-ins last 30 days. Sign in again and you're back where you were.
Tapped the wrong thing
Almost every action has an Undo right after, in the green bar or next to the item.
No phone alerts
Open App & alerts on that phone and make sure notifications are on. On iPhone, the app has to be opened from the Home Screen icon.
