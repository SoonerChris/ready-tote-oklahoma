App & alerts

Put the admin on your Home Screen like an app, and get a buzz when something needs you.

1. Home Screen app

You're in the app. Open it from the Ready Tote icon on your Home Screen any time.

On your iPhone, in Safari:

  1. Open this page while signed in.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Ready Tote from your Home Screen and come back here for step 2.

On Android, Chrome's menu has Install app or Add to Home screen.

2. Notifications

Checking this device…

Icon badge: checking…

Icon badge: the Ready Tote icon shows how many things are waiting on you: booking requests to handle plus late pickups. It updates when a notification comes in and when you open the app. To hide it, go to iPhone Settings, Notifications, Ready Tote, Badges.

Each phone or computer is set up on its own. Turning these on here doesn't change Shon's or anyone else's.

3. Inventory-only login

For a helper like Shon: they sign in with their own username and password and see only Inventory. They can log stock changes and mark totes clean. No customer phones, emails, addresses, prices or payments.

They sign in at the usual admin sign-in page. To change their password, save the same username with a new one. That signs them out everywhere until they use it.
